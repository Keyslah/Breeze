Breeze Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026

Breeze is an independently maintained Android application for personal overnight recording and historical monitoring. Its Android package is com.aaron.breeze. The project is maintained by Keyslah at github.com/Keyslah/Breeze.

This policy covers Breeze's features and its optional Oura connection in the History page. Oura access requires your authorization, and measurements are available only when Oura returns data for the selected recording.

Information stored on your device

Breeze stores its recordings and preferences in private Android app storage. Depending on the features you use, this includes:

  • Temperature and humidity readings, recording times, sensor and controller identifiers, fan-setting history, and connection or synchronization status.
  • Feeling entries you enter or dictate, including their times and ratings.
  • Your settings, named control profiles, graph preferences, and selected weather location.
  • Optional Dreo fan reports, temperature readings, and local diagnostic records.

The maintainer does not operate a server that receives your Breeze recording history. Breeze does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs and does not automatically upload its history or diagnostic logs to the maintainer.

Optional Oura connection

The connection uses the official Oura API V2, after you explicitly authorize Breeze through Oura's OAuth sign-in and consent flow. Breeze does not ask for or store your Oura password or connect directly to your ring.

Its purpose is to display these four measurements alongside existing overnight recordings:

  1. Nightly skin-temperature deviation from your personal baseline.
  2. Overnight heart-rate samples.
  3. Sleep-period average respiratory rate.
  4. Sleep stages.

The connection requests the daily permission needed for sleep and readiness records. Oura's permission covers more fields than Breeze needs; Breeze's import retains only the four measurements above and the metadata needed to identify, align, and update them, such as source record IDs, timestamps, time-zone offsets, sample intervals, units, and import status. Unneeded response fields are not retained as additional features.

Oura authorization tokens are protected on the device using Android Keystore-backed encryption. Imported history is stored separately from sensor rows in private app storage and remains available offline. It is used only for historical display, not predictions, correlations, recommendations, advertising, AI model training, or fan-control decisions. Breeze does not upload sensor history to Oura or send imported Oura readings to Dreo, weather services, or a maintainer-operated server.

Requests go directly from the device to Oura. Oura receives the authorization and API requests, including the requested date ranges and normal network information such as your IP address. Oura's processing of your account and original ring data remains governed by Oura's own privacy policy.

Other optional connections

Dreo: If you connect a Dreo account, Breeze sends your email address and a password-derived credential to Dreo over HTTPS to sign in and retrieve supported fan information. It stores the email, password-derived credential, access token, and selected device information encrypted with an Android Keystore key. The password-derived credential remains sensitive authentication information. Breeze can retain returned fan state, temperature, and synchronization history locally. Disconnecting Dreo removes the saved account credential envelope; it does not erase previously recorded history.

Outdoor weather: If you search for a place or fetch outdoor weather, Breeze sends the search text, or your selected location's coordinates and the requested date range, to Open-Meteo over HTTPS. It does not use GPS location permissions for this feature. Weather requests do not include your sensor readings, feeling entries, or Oura measurements.

These providers receive normal network information, including your IP address, when your device contacts them. Their services are governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

Microphone and local diagnostics

If you enable voice commands and grant microphone permission, speech recognition runs on the device using an offline model. Breeze does not send microphone audio to a cloud speech service or save raw microphone recordings. Recognized commands can create local feeling entries or perform the action you requested. Local Android diagnostic logs can contain recognized command text and device status. You can disable listening in Breeze or revoke its microphone permission in Android settings.

Sharing and exports

Breeze does not sell your data, use it for targeted advertising, or provide recording history to data brokers. Network sharing is limited to the optional service requests described above.

When you explicitly export existing Breeze history or feeling entries, Android lets you choose the destination. A destination you choose may be a cloud storage provider; that provider then handles the exported copy under its own policies. Copies you export or explicitly provide for troubleshooting are separate from the app's private storage. Do not post credentials or personal health recordings in the public source repository or its issue tracker.

Storage, retention, and your choices

Breeze retains local history to let you review saved nights; it does not apply an automatic time-based expiry to those recordings. The app disables Android's standard automatic app-data backup through its manifest. History databases are not separately encrypted by Breeze; Android's app isolation and device security protect private storage. Account credentials use the additional encryption described above.

You can stop optional connections or revoke Android permissions. For the Oura connection, withdrawing Breeze's authorization through Oura stops future authorized access; it does not by itself erase a previously imported local copy.

Android's app-storage controls or uninstalling Breeze can remove its local app data, including saved history, settings, and credentials. This removes all Breeze app data, so preserve any records you want before choosing that action. Exported copies, independently created development backups, data remaining on your controller, and data held by Oura or Dreo are not removed by clearing Breeze's local storage. Manage those copies with their respective holder or service. No data is deleted merely by reading this policy.

Policy updates and contact

The project website is hosted by GitHub Pages. It contains informational pages and an Oura sign-in return page. It does not provide an Oura password form, collect health readings, or use analytics scripts, tracking cookies, or third-party font services. GitHub receives ordinary web requests, including your IP address, when you visit the site; its handling of that information is governed by the GitHub Privacy Statement.

The return page uses a local script to hand an OAuth response directly to the Breeze Android package when you choose Return to Breeze. It does not exchange tokens, make API requests, or save responses in cookies or browser storage. The supported token response arrives in a URL fragment, which is not sent to the web host. The script removes the response from the current browser-history entry and keeps it temporarily in page memory. The return link expires after 5 minutes or sooner if the token expires; using the button or leaving the page discards the pending response. Query-string token and authorization-code responses are rejected; query strings, unlike fragments, are part of the ordinary web request received by the host. The Android integration also validates the sign-in request before accepting any response.

Changes to this policy will be published at this same address with an updated effective date. The repository's file history records prior versions.

For privacy questions, contact the maintainer through the Breeze issue tracker. Issues are public: describe the question without including passwords, tokens, or personal readings. If private details are necessary, first ask the maintainer to arrange a private contact method. The maintainer cannot access or delete data stored only on your phone remotely.
