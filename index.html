PERSONAL OVERNIGHT MONITORING

A clearer view
of your night.

Your room, your recordings, your observations. Breeze brings overnight sensor history and feeling entries together in one Android app.

Explore the project ↗
01

Keep the whole night

Review temperature, humidity, fan settings, and your own Hot or Cold entries along a shared history timeline.

02

Stay close to your data

Recordings live on your phone. Optional connections fetch the information you choose, and exports go to a destination you select.

See what is being built

Breeze is an independently maintained personal project. Its source and development history are available on GitHub.

IN DEVELOPMENT

Oura, alongside your recordings.

A planned optional connection will add four measurements to the existing History page: temperature deviation, overnight heart rate, respiratory average, and sleep stages.

Oura support is not available yet. It will require your authorization through Oura and is intended only for historical monitoring.
