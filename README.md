To rebuild and install firmware 1.2.14 from Windows (do not deploy during the investigation-only review):

1. Leave the Breeze controller powered and running normally.
2. Open PowerShell in this folder, run `./firmware/build-ota.ps1 -BuildPath ./firmware/out-auto-one-second-20261009 -VersionPatch 14`, then `./firmware/wireless-update.ps1 -FirmwarePath ./firmware/out-auto-one-second-20261009/BreezeController.ino.bin -SkipBuild`.
3. When prompted, hold the XIAO's built-in **BOOT** button for two seconds, then release it.
4. Wait for `Firmware ... was accepted`. The script reconnects the computer to its previous Wi-Fi network when it finishes.

The uploader validates its ESP32-C6 header, version marker, size, and SHA-256, temporarily joins `Breeze-OTA-...`, uploads to the inactive OTA slot, and removes the temporary Windows Wi-Fi profile. Home fits its live readings and controls on one screen. The top half uses History's colors and readout format for **D0**, **D1**, **D2**, **Highest body temp**, **SHT40 room temperature**, and **humidity**, without checkboxes. The bottom half has six manual fan buttons, **Logic**, **Start recording**, **Reconnect**, and an editable **Current fan setting** (0 = Off). Logic is blue when controller-confirmed active and purple when paused. Home buttons have no elevation animation or sending popup. Temperatures display in Fahrenheit. Routine Bluetooth readings and durable history samples arrive every 10 seconds; body sampling inside the controller stays at one second. The Fan page retains its manual learned IR commands, synchronization controls, and marked probe test. Setup contains automatic temperature rules with independent Warm/Cool room thresholds; the gap holds the current fan setting. Valid edits save automatically. Side-by-side **Save profile** and **Load profile** controls keep named copies on the phone and apply the chosen settings to the controller's single active profile. Automation requires a known current fan setting.

Home tracks confirmed app-controlled fan changes. If the original remote or physical buttons change the fan, correct **Current fan setting** and press the keyboard's **Done**. If an edited number is present when a fan button is tapped, Breeze sends the fan command first, waits for its acknowledgement, then synchronizes the resulting estimate. Normal known commands require no extra synchronization. Ambiguous command effects require explicit confirmation; infrared acknowledgements establish software transmission, not physical reception. Manual fan commands pause Logic; the Home **Logic** button resumes or pauses it without changing the fan's current setting.

After stopping a recording, Breeze downloads its records immediately when connected, or resumes automatically on the next Bluetooth connection. The controller reclaims only records committed to phone storage. The app recreates its Bluetooth connection after adapter restarts or stalled operations, retries an idle disconnected service, and restores foreground connection eligibility after unlocking. **Reconnect**, alongside Current fan setting on Home, immediately discards the old phone connection and reconnects to the saved controller without restarting the app or changing the controller's recording state. Home also shows the current connection status.

Bluetooth stays connected for 2 minutes after leaving Breeze or locking the screen, allowing a switch to Camera without interrupting troubleshooting. Returning before the deadline cancels the countdown and preserves the connection. After 2 minutes away, Breeze closes the connection and cancels retries; opening Breeze again reconnects and resumes downloads. The controller continues recording independently. In **Setup → Voice during recording**, **Disable listening** starts checked and remains saved across restarts. While checked, starting a recording, reconnecting, or tapping a previous listening action cannot turn on the microphone. Uncheck it explicitly to allow voice logging again.

On History, **Highest body temp** shows the highest available **D0, D1, or D2 body temperature** at each recorded timestamp. SHT40 room temperature is separate and never contributes to that maximum. Highest starts checked, with the D0, D1 and D2 traces unchecked; all numeric readings remain available below the graph. The highest default is applied once on upgrade, preserving weather/humidity choices and subsequent checkbox changes. **Fan setting** is enabled by default as a stepped line on this same plot, with levels 0 (Off), 1–9, and H; it does not add another graph or change the temperature range. Installed firmware 1.2.10 records known fan settings every ten seconds. Older nights have no fan-setting data and stay Unknown. Older nights without D2 use the available D0/D1 readings, and missing body readings remain gaps. Pinch the recorded-night graph to zoom, drag horizontally to pan, and use Reset zoom or double-tap to see the whole night. The temperature axis fits only enabled traces in the visible time window, using fractional degree labels for small changes. Add a feeling entry with its date/time, Hot or Cold, and intensity 1–10; tap a saved entry to edit it. Orange Hot triangles and blue Cold circles mark entries on the graph and show their time/rating when tapped. Phone history and controller sensor rows are retained by an additive database migration.
